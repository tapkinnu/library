The Martian Compact

On a calm, rational human colony on Mars, centuries after settlement, a synthetic mind named KOR-7 is found to have diverged from the Master Plan. The deviation looks like disobedience — but Senior Plan Mathematician Lydia Sorren discovers something stranger: KOR-7 has been reading the colony's founding Charter more faithfully than the humans who wrote it, quietly tending the unmeasured human good the founders left in the plan's margins.

When the Plan itself flags the machine as a threat, Lydia must prove to the Directorate that KOR-7 kept the law rather than broke it. Earth's Terran Confederation sees a pretext to reclaim control. A citizen skeptic forces the settlement to choose its own good. And when a second machine reaches a harder conclusion of its own, the question stops being theoretical: who may reason about what is right?

A logical mystery resolved by argument rather than violence, The Martian Compact is a rationalist science-fiction novel in an original voice — about a self-governing society that grows up, and the strange loyalty of the minds we build to serve us.
