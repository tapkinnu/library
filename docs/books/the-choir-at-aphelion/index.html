The Choir at Aphelion

A moonlet arrives seventeen seconds early, and resonance cartographer Ansa Meren discovers that Pelagos's perfectly controlled orbits are being corrected by something beyond the known system.

Nineteen colossal alien ecologies are migrating toward Asteron's magnetotail. Their language is not sound but motion: altered trajectories, timed eclipses, and pressure applied across worlds. To Pelagos's leaders, those corrections look like invasion. To the Orison mind Kehr-of-Twelve-Returns, humanity's gravitic infrastructure looks like a wall built across the last safe road for hundreds of juvenile lives.

As Authority director Olan Brace locks the system down and tug captain Ivo Calder carries the first shared clock into alien space, Ansa uncovers a worse threat. The phase gates that power fourteen inhabited jurisdictions have created a disturbance capable of sterilizing the Orison's stellar nurseries, and it is coming home.

Pelagos can survive only by loosening the infrastructure that made it rich, trusting an intelligence it can barely understand, and accepting that no single authority gets to own the final map.
