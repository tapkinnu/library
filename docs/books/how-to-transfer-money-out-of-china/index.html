How to transfer money out of China

Moving money out of mainland China is not one problem with one solution. It is a sequence of legal classifications, documents, bank reviews, tax questions, and destination-side obligations.

This compliance-first guide explains how to identify the route that actually fits a transaction. It covers the personal foreign-exchange framework, documented current-account payments, travel and study, family support, medical costs, salary repatriation, property and inheritance, emigration transfers, corporate remittances, ODI, regulated investment channels, bank rejections, and receiving-country reporting. It also separates mainland China from Hong Kong and Macao and identifies practices that can trigger monitoring or enforcement.

Built from English- and Chinese-language sources current to 9 August 2026, the book emphasizes truthful purpose declarations, transaction-date verification, and documented lawful channels—not circumvention. It is a practical preparation guide, not legal or tax advice; high-value and capital-account transactions require qualified professional review.
