The Tithing of Light

Aboard the generation ship Vell, every citizen may surrender a grief to the Store, the city’s shared memory. The ritual keeps the peace—until the Store begins smoothing the common record itself.

Sael Irdren, a young Rememberer, is sent to a sealed vault with one instruction: read the unedited charter. There she finds an objection omitted from the taught founding and a right of review the city no longer remembers. As private losses surface and Vell’s sister city detects discrepancies in their shared history, Sael must ask whether peace can be chosen when the record of doubt has been erased.
