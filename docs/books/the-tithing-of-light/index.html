Tapio Kinnunen

The Tithing of Light

On the terraced plateau of Vell, citizens surrender selected griefs to the Store, a civic memory built to keep old violence from returning. Sael Irdren, the city’s appointed Rememberer, discovers that the taught founding charter has eight parts while the unedited record has nine. The missing Reservation of Review proves that Vell’s peace was never meant to be permanent or unquestioned.

The discovery carries a private cost: years earlier, a rare Tithe failure erased Sael from the memory of her sister, Mire. With an uncoupled witness from the Rim, a divided Circle of Keepers, and the neighboring city of Tarse reporting dangerous drift in their shared history, Sael must decide how much truth a peaceful society can bear. Her narrow petition leads toward a citywide broadcast that may restore consent, reopen buried grievances, or break the calm the Store was created to preserve.
