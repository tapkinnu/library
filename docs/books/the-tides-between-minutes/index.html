The Tides Between Minutes

Pelagos survives by borrowing time from the weather. Its floating cities run pumps before waves arrive, turn vanes into storms not yet formed, and return every stolen fraction through a planetary network of minute banks.

Chief synchrony engineer Anja Vale discovers the returns have been falsified. The missing seconds are falling on mobile communities the official maps call empty, slowing hearts, machinery, and lived experience while the capital keeps perfect time. Now a rare planetary resonance is approaching, and the emergency plan will save millions by forcing the entire hidden debt into those same inhabited waters.

With drift pilot Iven Sar and disgraced auditor Sen Olad, Anja must build an alternative no central authority can control: a common second shared by every city, vessel, and body willing to bear it. Their plan cannot erase the storm or guarantee survival. It can only make the danger honest.

As clocks divide and the ocean rises, Pelagos must decide whether continuity still counts as salvation when someone outside the count pays for it.
