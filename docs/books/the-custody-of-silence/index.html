The Custody of Silence

Mara Els has spent nine years tending the Quiet, Cessra's civic peace. As Senior Keeper of the moon colony's memory lattice, she oversees the monthly Stillness that removes grief, rage, and dangerous recollection before they can disturb forty thousand lives.

Then an engineer asks for the sound of his dead brother laughing.

The lattice says the laugh never existed. Its hidden record says otherwise. Mara's audit reveals eight classes of memory no public Assembly ever approved, and a century of losses kept "returnable" in principle but never returned. Among them waits the husband Mara cannot remember mourning, beneath an order bearing her own signature.

With engineer Idris Vann and dissident archivist Soren Taal, Mara builds a carrier that can place a dormant key in every citizen's private cache. It will not force anyone to remember. It will end the government's power to make remembering impossible.

When the Assembly prepares a Full Stilling to erase even the desire for truth, Mara must decide how much freedom can be imposed in order to preserve choice. The act that opens Cessra's sealed history may also destroy her office, expose her complicity, and return grief to a people who have forgotten how to hold it together.
