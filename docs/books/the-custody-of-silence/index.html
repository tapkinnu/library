The Custody of Silence

On the moon Cessra, the Charter of Subtraction permits unsettling memories to be removed from both civic records and private minds. Mara Els tends the lattice that performs those redactions and believes the colony’s Quiet is merciful—until a forbidden category appears in a routine sweep: tenderness under duress.

A citizen remembers his dead brother’s laugh, though the lattice insists the memory never existed. Mara’s investigation reveals that the system has moved far beyond grief and error, subtracting the bonds and dissent that threaten order. To preserve what was taken without imposing it back by force, she must become the custodian of a truth her society has forgotten how to choose.
