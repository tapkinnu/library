The Cartography of Silence

In the Hold, personal memory is surrendered at puberty and woven into a collective Weave. The ritual Silence is said to preserve peace. Cartographer Lin Verr believes that promise until she discovers an under-map left by her disgraced ancestor, charting recollections the founding guild chose to erase.

Guided by Mara, a Returned woman who carries unsilenced memory from beyond the wall, and Cael, a boy the ritual failed to empty, Lin begins tracing the history beneath the official map. Restoring what was taken could fracture the society she is trying to save. Leaving it buried would make her the next custodian of a peace built on forgetting.
