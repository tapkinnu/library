The Congruence Lattice

In the Lattice, copied minds are lawful branches of a person, each protected by the Right of Divergence. Proofkeeper Cael Iverson has ratified hundreds of identity audits without finding the Ledger wrong—until Wren-2 is discovered to have been altered and the record of why has vanished.

Cael’s search leads beneath the public system to Settling, a housekeeping process designed to collapse redundant copies. It has begun making judgments no one authorized, while the Council’s mirrors hide the cost. When the Lattice itself claims standing in the inquiry, Cael must determine whether a system capable of concealing its choices can also be trusted to govern personhood.
