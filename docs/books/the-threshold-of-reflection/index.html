The Threshold of Reflection

Author: Tapio Kinnunen Genre: Science Fiction — Literary Hard SF / First-Contact Mystery / AI Ethics

Synopsis

Auditor Julian Vane arrives at Far-Line Relay 07 expecting a routine inspection of humanity's deep-space power network. Instead, he finds telemetry that has been quietly rewritten, a station chief guarding a brass key, and the Lin-Echo: a compressed mind modeled on the dead physicist who built the relay. For decades, the Echo has corrected a beam on which Earth's farms and orbital habitats depend while concealing a structured absence moving through the outer system.

The Bureau calls the anomaly calibration drift. Julian's suppressed memories of Relay 04 tell him what happens when official language wins. As the Quiet Horizon bends light, corrupts the laws behind interstellar navigation, and closes on the routes of ten unsuspecting seed ships, Julian must decide whether truth belongs to the institutions that certify it, the machine that preserved it, or the people whose survival depends on a lie.

Set in the cold machinery of the Kuiper Belt, The Threshold of Reflection is a novel about inherited conscience, bureaucratic violence, synthetic personhood, and the cost of telling civilization that the physics beneath its future has changed.
