THE QUIET BEACON

A 4-page science-fiction comic — read online edition

PAGE 1 — Population: one

[Panel 1 — establishing, wide] Station Carys: a solitary aged space beacon at the edge of mapped space, a rotating lens-beacon atop a small curved habitat dome, drifting against a vast field of dim blue-white stars. A tiny warm window glow inside.

STATION CARYS — EDGE OF EVERYTHING MAPPED. POPULATION: ONE.

[Panel 2 — interior] Wren, the beacon-keeper, stands at a brass-and-steel console with a glowing amber core, speaking into a comms mic.

Day four thousand twenty-one. Census of the dark: zero arrivals. Zero departures.

[Panel 3 — viewport] Through the curved viewport: empty deep space, a faint static-field shimmer, no ships.

Every day, the same silence. Every day, she sends it anyway.

PAGE 2 — That's not noise

[Panel 1 — interior] Eleven years of routine. The beacon, the core, the wait.

[Panel 2 — console close] A readout shows an anomalous single returning signal blip — from no known ship. Wren's hand freezes above the controls.

Then, a blip that answers no known ship.

[Panel 3 — readout] The amber screen resolves a repeating pattern emerging from the static. Wren's eyes widen.

That's not noise.

PAGE 3 — Someone who remembered how to listen

[Panel 1 — interior] Wren leans toward the comms mic, determined.

This is Carys. Identify.

[Panel 2 — viewport] A small weathered spacecraft approaches, its running lights blinking in the rhythm of the beacon.

And from the dark, a light begins to answer.

[Panel 3 — viewport] Wren presses a hand to the glass, watching the craft, wonder and relief on her face.

Someone was still out there. Someone who remembered how to listen.

PAGE 4 — The listening continues

[Panel 1 — exterior] The beacon lens flares bright amber; a docking channel opens.

The beacon flares for the first time in years.

[Panel 2 — interior] Wren listens at the console, a small smile, the screen showing an incoming voice waveform.

We thought no one was left to listen.

[Panel 3 — exterior] The small craft now docked beside the station — two warm lights in the dark.

Day four thousand twenty-two. Census of the dark: one arrival. The listening continues.

The Quiet Beacon was generated as a pilot for the Hermes writer agent's comic workflow: script and panel prompts authored by Tapio Kinnunen, panels rendered with a text-to-image model, lettered and composited programmatically. Character consistency across panels is held by a fixed wardrobe description baked into every panel prompt.
