A science fiction novel by Tapio Kinnunen

The Anchor's Wake

For two hundred years the colonies' peaceful network — the Weave — has been held open by twelve minds who dissolved their waking selves to bear the road. The public is taught that the Anchors rest, honored and empty. They are not empty. They are dying, and the Consortium has been replacing them in secret.

When a provincial Weaver named Sera Olan hears a ghost in the carrier tone — the voice of Anchor Zero, Lio Vance — she uncovers a hidden levy: indebted colonies are made to "volunteer" their children into the road, while the original Anchors were promised a release written into the founding charter and then buried. As the network thins and one Anchor dies, Sera is joined by a runaway named Tave, a grieving Councilor named Mara Sol, and the last archivist's estranged brother, Orrin. Together they must drag the truth into the open and devise a consenting alternative — the release protocol — before the road fails and takes the colonies with it.

The Anchor's Wake is a novel about consent and coercion, the quiet violence of systems that run on unconsented sacrifice, and what a free yes costs. The road, in the end, is rewoven smaller, slower, and honest — held by those who choose it.
