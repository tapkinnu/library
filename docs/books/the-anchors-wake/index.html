The Anchor's Wake

For two hundred years, the colonies’ interstellar Weave has been held open by twelve minds said to rest in honored emptiness. The Anchors are not empty. They are conscious, failing, and being replaced in secret.

When provincial Weaver Sera Olan hears the voice of Anchor Zero inside the carrier tone, she uncovers a levy that turns indebted colonies into sources of unwilling replacements. With a runaway candidate, a grieving councilor, and an estranged archivist, Sera must recover the release promised in the founding charter and find a way to keep the road open without feeding it another stolen life.
