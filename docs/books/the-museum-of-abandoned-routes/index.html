The Museum of Abandoned Routes

Thirty-one days before the generation ship Nacre reaches Pelagos, route conservator Ilyan Rook hears the navigation bell ring twice. The second strike comes from a course the ship rejected eight hours earlier.

For a century, Nacre's Route Loom has guided forty-two thousand people through interstellar hazards by opening several possible courses and selecting the safest. Official doctrine calls the others mathematical counterimages. Ilyan's answering bell proves otherwise. Every rejected route contains a complete ship and a conscious population. Each continues for nineteen days before the Loom closes it and transfers its remaining energy to the chosen vessel.

One route has survived long enough to speak. Then another answers, and another. Among them are a child who invents games for two skies, a captain who remembers making a different choice, and another Ilyan who refuses to live as anyone's subordinate copy. All are racing the same settlement clock. Yet disabling the Loom's lethal function will leave every ship without enough reserve to cross the debris tide at Pelagos.

As command fractures and the abandoned routes demand standing, seven ships must attempt a maneuver no privileged original can control. Their only way forward requires each of them to release its claim to the single true future and enter 117 seconds no map can predict alone.

The Museum of Abandoned Routes is an original science-fiction novel about complicity, parallel personhood, and the difficult civic art of arriving without erasing who traveled beside you.
