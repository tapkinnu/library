The Glass Monument

Across the continents, immense towers of vitrified silicate have been treated as geological facts for longer than recorded history. Graduate researcher Kirin Varel discovers that Monument 7 is not inert stone but a compressed archive carrying thousands of years of human and alien memory.

The discovery draws Kirin into a conflict between dormant minds known as the Architects and the human Glass Walkers who have resisted them for generations. As the Monument’s thirty-eight-year Return approaches, preservation begins to look less like rescue and more like surrender. Kirin must decide whether humanity’s future belongs inside the archive—or whether remaining human requires accepting the risks of flesh, uncertainty, and time.
