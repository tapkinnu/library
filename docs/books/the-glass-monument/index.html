The Glass Monument

Author: Tapio Kinnunen Genre: Science Fiction — Hard SF / Archaeological Mystery / Philosophical SF

Synopsis

In a world where giant monolithic "Monuments" of vitrified silicate pierce the continental crust, humanity treats them as unchanging geological features—until graduate student Kirin Varel discovers that Monument 7 is not inert stone, but a compressed neural archive holding seven thousand years of human and alien memory. As the 38-year cycle of the "Return" ticks down, Kirin is drawn into an underground consensus of dormant minds known as the Architects and their ancient human adversaries, the Glass Walkers. Working between university politics, underground field stations, and the silent towers themselves, Kirin must determine whether humanity should upload into the crystalline archive to survive a cosmic vacuum decay wave or find a third path of non-collapsing measurement that preserves the vulnerable flesh of living beings.

Spanning Antarctica's frozen wastes to ancient archives, The Glass Monument is an epic meditation on deep time, preservation, consciousness, and the choices that define what it means to remain human.
