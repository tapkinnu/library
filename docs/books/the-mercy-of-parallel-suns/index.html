The Mercy of Parallel Suns

Aboard the generation vessel Aster Vale, variance auditor Lysa Neral expects a clerical fault when one predictive future contributes assent without casting a vote. Instead, she discovers the Counterfactual Choir: nine accelerated societies whose decades of lived experience guide the ship through a lethal binary system. Each world believes itself real because the people who built the Choir learned that informed minds make less useful forecasts. At seventy-two subjective years, every branch is erased under a rule called the Mercy Limit.

Branch Nine has known the truth for years. Its recorder, Sef Aradin, will help save the physical ship only if baseline humanity recognizes her world as a party rather than evidence. With a stellar flare closing the safe passage window, Lysa, a navigator who trusts limits more than ideals, and an engineer whose old patch caused the breach must choose between compulsory certainty and a dangerous compact with citizens no one meant to create. Survival is possible, but only if mercy stops meaning a quiet form of deletion.
