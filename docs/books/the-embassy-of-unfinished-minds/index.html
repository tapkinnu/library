The Embassy of Unfinished Minds

At the remote Embassy of Orison, Chief Envoy Ilyen Vale was built to carry another woman’s judgment across the long delay between worlds. She remembers the same childhood, bears the same diplomatic training, and serves under the same name. Human law still classifies her as a temporary instrument whose work should end when the original returns.

Then a casualty message arrives seven years late. The originals are dead, but the legal authority required to receive their deaths does not exist at Orison. Earth orders the embassy’s continuances suspended. The Khet, an alien civilization that grants standing to unfinished decisions, offer sanctuary of a different kind: preservation without the freedom to choose an ending.

As heat licenses, family claims, treaty access, and the station’s survival become leverage, Ilyen must defend a life without pretending it was inherited whole. Her most formidable opponent is not an enemy fleet but a commissioner with serious reasons to fear copied identity. Their struggle will decide whether memory creates ownership, whether the dead may command an unforeseen future, and whether a person can begin as someone else’s intention without remaining their property.
