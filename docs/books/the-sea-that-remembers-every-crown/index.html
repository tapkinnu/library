The Sea That Remembers Every Crown

Every public promise spoken over the Sarravai Sea enters the water. Kept oaths steady currents; broken promises gather into crownstorms that raise drowned roads and drive ships toward unpaid debts.

When the Nine-Harbor oath breaks at low tide, orc wake-judge Hessa Kord hears abandoned villages inside the storm. The evidence points to Tide Chancellor Varo Senn, whose Hush Armada quiets the water by erasing conquered names and poisoning living reefs.

Hessa crosses the sea aboard the Mothwake alongside an elven cartographer concealing an old crime, a fleet captain haunted by abandoned refugees, and a goblin hullwright protecting a floating city. To reach the First Shore, they must unite orc confederacies, elven root-courts, and refugee fleets before seven crownstorms decide whether the living must obey every promise their ancestors made.
