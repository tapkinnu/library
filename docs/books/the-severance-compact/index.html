The Severance Compact

In the thirty-fourth year of the Compact, archivist Enna Solven finds an unauthorized thaw-read made with credentials belonging to her mother, dead for twelve years. The accessed crystal holds the founders’ sealed debate over a protocol that engineered peace by suppressing humanity’s inherited memory of war.

Those memories are returning, and the Council has spent decades hiding the recurrence. Enna’s search uncovers falsified statistics, encrypted marginalia, and archivists who preserved what the official record forbids. Broadcasting the evidence could destabilize the civilization her mother helped protect. Keeping it sealed would make peace dependent on another generation’s ignorance.
