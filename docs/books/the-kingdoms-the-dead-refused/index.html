The Kingdoms the Dead Refused

Sixty-three years ago the Solcar Dominion conquered Nareth by planting seven ironwood standards in mass graves. Their roots preserved each battlefield’s last hour, the trenches, commands, and the trained motion of the unburied dead. The empire called it victory; the living built farms, gardens, and mixed cities over the silence. Now the emperor has died, and at Cinder Vale the ground stands up.

Orc grave-speaker Vara Kesh saves three thousand eight hundred and forty villagers from ranks formed of soil and old violence. Among them marches Rusk, the brother she knows only from family stories; his battle-echo keeps turning aside to shield an enemy child. In the capital, human surveyor Deren Vale watches present roads vanish beneath remembered trenches, while elf scent-archivist Sivei Ardan discovers her court omitted the laborers whose graves feed its wealth. Goblin engineer Orrik Fen proves that ironwood cannot be safely destroyed without unleashing every stored battle-hour at once.

Their answer begins as a funeral caravan across rain stairs, salt canopies, ruined forts, and river cities where restoration armies want to command the dead against former conquerors. Regent Ysara Pell survived the reprisals after the armistice and will not let Nareth divide into purges and rival crowns, but her Concord of One Hour would bind all seven standards into one permanent guardian host. At the Field Without Dawn, victory must give the dead a reason to refuse every kingdom.
