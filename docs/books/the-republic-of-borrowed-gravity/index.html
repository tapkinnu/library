The Republic of Borrowed Gravity

A missing 0.0037 g in an audit drum leads forensic cartographer Iona Rhyll to a truth the Forty-Three Systems Republic has hidden inside its most ordinary miracle: gravity does not come free. Every habitat kept stable by Reciprocal Mass Exchange exports an equal wound to a system certified empty.

Eidos is not empty. Beneath its ice, the Olun preserve minds in pressure histories, and a century of human debt pulses has shattered their nurseries and erased lineages no telescope knew how to see. When the Olun begin returning those debts, cities lose weight, ships fall out of controlled flight, and billions discover that survival rests on an invisible extermination.

With heavy-lift captain Tavin Serr, an alien speaker called Aru-in-Pressure, and the evidence her dead sister tried to expose, Iona must build a third choice between immediate collapse and continued atrocity. But an honest orbit will cost fuel, homes, political unity, and a ship carrying the people who refuse to let harm disappear into a blank column.
