Cinder Nine: The Last Gun — Script Edition

By Tapio Kinnunen

Page 1 — THE LINE BREAKS

(Three panels: wide banner, two lower halves)

Panel 1A: Deep space above a cracked moon orbiting a swollen red giant. A battered human fleet retreats while sleek black organic crescent ships tear through the defensive line. The dreadnought Unbroken shields twelve luminous civilian arks. Caption: CINDER NINE. HUMANITY'S LAST FORTRESS BEFORE THE REFUGEE CORRIDOR.

Panel 1B: Bridge close-up. Mara stands amid sparks, one hand on the command rail, her amber cybernetic eye flickering. Behind her, the tactical display is almost entirely red. Officer: "The line is gone."

Panel 1C: A Kharax boarding claw punches through the Unbroken's hull. Inside the breach, a towering warrior unfolds into the corridor through smoke. Mara (comms): "Then we become the line."

Page 2 — ELEVEN MINUTES

(Three panels)

Panel 2A: Human marines fire down a narrow corridor as Kharax charge across walls and ceiling. The defense is clearly collapsing. Caption: THE KHARAX TAKE WHAT A NERVOUS SYSTEM REMEMBERS.

Panel 2B: Inside one refugee ark, families strap in under dim blue lights. An eight-year-old child presses both hands to a round viewport, watching the battle flare behind them. Child: "Are they coming with us?"

Panel 2C: Mara and Sol at a fractured tactical table. A clean projection shows the arks, the moon Ash, the red giant, and the converging brood. Sol: "The arks need eleven minutes." Mara: "We have four."

Page 3 — THE BURIED WEAPON

(Two stacked wide panels)

Panel 3A: The cracked moon Ash fills the frame. In a canyon, immense metallic ribs form a weapon aimed directly at the red giant. Caption: ASH MOON. THE HELIOS LANCE — ABANDONED BECAUSE FIRING IT WOULD KILL ITS CREW.

Panel 3B: Mara faces Sol in the bridge's emergency amber light. The moon-gun glows on the viewports behind them. Mara: "The brood follows the strongest reactor." Sol: "Ours."

Page 4 — THE PRICE

(Three panels)

Panel 4A: The Unbroken has physically docked to the moon-gun. Human engineers cross an exposed gantry while Kharax ships rake the moon with cyan fire. Caption: MINUTE SEVEN.

Panel 4B: Sol kneels at the targeting core, red harness clipped to an escape spindle. He looks up at Mara and realizes what she has done. Sol: "There are two seats." Mara: "There was one pilot."

Panel 4C: Through the canyon mouth, hundreds of Kharax crescents turn as one toward the Unbroken's brightening reactor. Caption: THE BROOD SMELLS THE FIRE.

Page 5 — THE FEINT

(Three panels)

Panel 5A: The Unbroken pulls away from the moon-gun and charges toward the red giant, reactor blazing. The Kharax swarm abandons the arks and follows. Caption: MINUTE NINE. ONE SHIP RUNS TOWARD THE SUN.

Panel 5B: Mara alone on the bridge, flying through debris. Her cybernetic eye overlays the moon-gun's firing corridor. Mara: "Come on, then."

Panel 5C: Sol in the moon-gun sees the arks approach their jump aperture while the swarm enters the marked corridor. Sol: "Solution forming — eighty percent."

Page 6 — THE HANDOFF

(Two stacked wide panels)

Panel 6A: Sol reaches toward Mara's hologram as the harness locks around him. Mara's gloved hand is on a remote eject control aboard the Unbroken. Sol: "Mara — don't." Mara: "Carry the light."

Panel 6B: Sol's escape spindle blasts free of the moon as the Unbroken locks into the gun's emitter cradle. The Kharax swarm nearly fills the firing corridor. Caption: SOLUTION: COMPLETE. ARK JUMP: COMPLETE.

Page 7 — THE LAST GUN

(Full-page splash)

Panel 7: The Helios Lance fires through the Unbroken into the red giant. A controlled solar prominence becomes a white-amber spear engulfing the Kharax swarm. The human ship is a tiny black silhouette at the heart of the light. Caption: FOR ELEVEN MINUTES, CINDER NINE HELD.

Page 8 — WHAT SURVIVES

(Two stacked wide panels)

Panel 8A: Sol floats bruised but alive inside the recovered escape spindle, now docked in an ark hangar. Mara's final amber recording glows on the canopy. Mara (recording): "Tell them Cinder Nine held."

Panel 8B: The same child from page 2 stands at the ark viewport. Far behind, Cinder Nine's flare looks like a new dawn. The ark convoy flies toward a field of stars. Caption: AND HUMANITY CARRIED ITS LIGHT INTO THE DARK.

End.
