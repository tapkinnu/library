Cinder Nine: The Last Gun

The Kharax have broken the last human line. Twelve refugee arks need eleven minutes to escape into the dark, and only the battered dreadnought Cinder Nine remains between them and the swarm.

Commander Mara Venn has one forbidden weapon, a failing reactor, and a crew who know exactly what their orders will cost. As the evacuation clock falls toward zero, she must decide how much of the ship—and of herself—can be spent to buy strangers a future.

With the Kharax closing from every direction, Mara turns a desperate retreat into a final tactical gamble: lure the swarm where one last gun can reach it, and trust someone else to carry the story forward.
