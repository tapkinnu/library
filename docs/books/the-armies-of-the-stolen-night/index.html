The Armies of the Stolen Night

Night in Avarra is alive. Every region’s darkness grows from sleepers, the dead, moths, fungi, weather, and the creatures that hunt between sunset and dawn. For forty years the Solar Empire has imprisoned those local Nights beneath the Sun Basilica, promising safe roads and an end to night-born plague; the conquered lands receive an unsetting sun, seeds that will not open, children who cannot dream, and armies whose stolen shadows fight beside them.

When imperial mirror riders take the Night of Seven Camp, orc night-reeve Zarha Kesh saves most of her people but cannot wake two hundred and eighty dreamless sleepers. Human refugee marshal Kessa Dorn turns an evacuation into rebellion with nineteen stolen water carts. In the capital, elf astronomer Ilyen Vaor discovers eleven captive Nights already gathered beneath the basilica.

Fifty-three days before a total eclipse, Empress Caldra Ves intends to bind a twelfth Night and forge one mobile imperial darkness whose shadow armies could end every war. Their road crosses the Red Salt, rival orc Night Courts, the blackglass foundries of Karth Veil, and a dying elf city. Goblin engineer Pikk Rill built the mirrors that bind shade-names and carries the fracture key that can free every shadow. At the eclipse, four wounded leaders must persuade twelve communities to host darkness together, asking whether imperial peace is worth becoming the catastrophe Caldra herself once survived.
