The Conservatory of Broken Seasons

On the tidally locked world of Meral, spring is not a gift of nature. It is scheduled by orbital mirrors, balanced against cloud, cold, and the heat a narrow civilization can safely shed into permanent night.

Seasonal auditor Senn Vale discovers that Orison's celebrated bloom has been purchased through an unnamed winter debit. For twenty-two years, three darkward communities have absorbed lethal cold while the law counts their homes, clinics, and schools as pieces of meteorological equipment. Exposing the transfer is easy compared with ending it: cancel the debit too quickly and the mirror lattice could drive a shear storm across the inhabited ribbon.

With a darkward engineer, a keeper of forbidden address ledgers, and the official who once chose managed harm over famine, Senn must build a remedy that does not turn another community into the next sacrifice. Their argument will decide whether weather can remain property, whether survival excuses inherited injustice, and what every inhabited place is owed before comfort can be traded.
