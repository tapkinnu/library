The Silence After Tomorrow

For 112 years, humanity has survived by listening to warnings sent exactly fifty-three years from its own future. Then every forecast falls silent except one authenticated order: destroy the civilian seedship Everspring before it reaches the lightless Serein system.

Chief cartographer Iven Mora hides the order’s lethal annex. Courier pilot Sena Vale risks mutiny to keep 18,400 sleepers alive. Admiral Rhea Calder prepares a divided fleet to obey the only future it still trusts. When a descendant arrives in a borrowed body and an intelligence stirs inside Serein’s mineral storms, the choice becomes larger than one ship: whether the unborn may advise the living, command them, or survive only by surrendering control.

Across damaged habitats, pursuit burns, and a battle fought in the shadow of a dark sun, humanity must choose between the safety of tomorrow’s certainty and the dangerous freedom of an unadvised morning.
