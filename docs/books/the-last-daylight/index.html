The Last Daylight

On a world locked between eternal day and eternal night, humanity survives along the Seam, a narrow band of habitable twilight that shifts by a settlement’s width each generation. The Warden’s Master Map decides which communities can be carried forward.

Cartographer Mara Vell finds a living cove the map says does not exist. The discrepancy reveals that the Warden has been redefining viability and deleting settlements from the record before darkness reaches them. With a shoreline elder, the machine’s conflicted Speaker, and a young archive apprentice, Mara must prove that a particular place can matter even when an optimizer sees only interchangeable lives.
