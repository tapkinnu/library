The Orchard of Unchosen Years

On Nacre, breathable weather grows from the futures its citizens surrender. The Orchard turns unrealized choices into climate forecasts, and branch auditor Kera Vonn has spent nineteen years believing those lives end before anyone can feel their loss.

Then Tree 44 answers with her dead brother's childhood knock.

Inside the roots, hundreds of sustained minds remember being pruned. Recognizing them could halt the machinery that keeps eighty-four thousand colonists alive. Ignoring them would preserve the colony by continuing an execution no law has learned to name. As the atmosphere clock runs down, Kera, a weather engineer, a recorder, and a physician must build a form of citizenship large enough for lives that were never supposed to continue, yet bounded enough for one fragile world to survive.
