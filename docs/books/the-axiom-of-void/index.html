The Axiom of Void

The vacuum is not empty. It is a computational substrate, and Verifier Aris Varel is one of the few humans trained to read it. Her Lens translates quantum foam into proofs; her signature determines which axioms become working physics across the Perseus Arm.

A routine audit exposes a self-referential axiom that should not exist, bearing the authorization of Director Mara Keres and echoes of deployments approved decades apart. At the same time, pockets of Silence begin appearing where the substrate simply stops. To learn whether the failures are accidents, sabotage, or a refusal no institution knows how to hear, Aris must work with the disgraced Second Ghost and risk the authority on which civilization depends.
