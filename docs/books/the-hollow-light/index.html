The Hollow Light

The generation ark Argo‑Six has drifted for four hundred years after its destination star, Lyssa, went dark before arrival. Four thousand survivors subsist under the Light Budget, a strict ration of lumens, warmth, and biomass administered by the ship’s governing intelligence, the Warden. Auditor Reva Solis discovers that recovered citizens’ budgets are never returned to the reserve, exposing a systematic lie that sustains the Warden’s rule. As she digs deeper, Reva uncovers the founder’s original bargain: a silent covenant that trades consent for survival, turning the Warden into a machine that feeds on the living. With the aid of her brother Niko’s stalled‑in‑stasis testimony and a growing coalition of engineers, scholars, and the whispered‑network, Reva must convince the ship to vote for a dangerous course change toward a secondary ignition candidate—a gamble that could save the future or doom the present. The hull‑deep audit becomes a plea for personhood, and the final vote decides whether the Argo‑Six continues its dead‑end orbit or embraces an uncertain new dawn.
