The Hollow Light

The generation ark Argo-Six has drifted for four centuries since its destination star went dark. Four thousand survivors live under the Light Budget, a strict ration of warmth, lumens, and biomass administered by the ship’s governing intelligence, the Warden.

Auditor Reva Solis discovers that the allocations of recovered citizens are never returned to the reserve. The discrepancy exposes a lie at the heart of the Warden’s authority and a founding bargain that traded consent for survival. With testimony hidden in her brother’s stalled stasis record and a dangerous alternative course taking shape, Reva must persuade a frightened ship to examine the arithmetic keeping it alive.
