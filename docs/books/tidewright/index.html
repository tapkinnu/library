The Tidewright's Compass

On Aurelune, a tidally locked ocean world, the sea is a living network called the Choir. For nine generations, the Concord has managed it by dredging channels, suppressing luminous blooms, and optimizing the water for human trade.

Tidewright Osa Vael reads the bloom-glyphs and currents by which the Choir speaks. When the sea begins forming one recurring spiral, she recognizes the same signal her late sister heard before being silenced: attention. With a cyclic Long Night approaching, the Concord tightens its control. Osa must decide whether survival depends on better prediction—or on yielding enough authority to listen to a world humanity has mistaken for a resource.
