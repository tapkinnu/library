Tapio Kinnunen

The Shepherds of Empty Space

Kessa Rho has spent her life cutting dead machinery away from the fixed gravitational routes that hold thirty-eight human systems together. Then an anchor moves beneath her hands.

The “hollows” inside the network are not natural knots in space. They are the captive young of colossal migratory animals, pinned for generations so human ships can cross between stars in hours. Now an adult is returning, twelve injured juveniles are trying to break free, and every attempt to hold the routes open stores more violence for the inhabited systems around them.

With a civilian convoy carrying 410,000 people and seventy million tonnes of survival infrastructure stranded in the chain, Kessa must turn a salvage tug, divided route crews, and the fleet sent to stop her into something humanity has never needed before: shepherds in a wilderness their roads were built to wound.

Freedom for the migration will end the routes. Keeping them may destroy the worlds they were meant to connect.
