The Ministry of Unfallen Stars

For 143 years, accordant gates have bound forty-seven human worlds into a single civilization. Every crossing carries food, medicine, families, and a hidden cost recorded only as harmless residue.

Auditor Talia Sorn discovers the residue has been living.

When the carrier Morrow Glass arrives twice and leaves behind Isca Ren, a woman from a Kest that history never chose, an empty reserve star begins speaking with nine million voices. The Ministry orders those voices erased before panic closes the routes that keep entire planets alive. Talia, carrier captain Rhen Tal, and Isca must build an alternative from the machinery of the crime itself: a slower network that refuses to bury its discarded futures.

But saving the unfallen may strand the present, and the easiest rescue would overwrite another inhabited world.

The Ministry of Unfallen Stars is an original science-fiction novel about logistics, personhood, secrecy, and the futures a civilization teaches itself not to count.
