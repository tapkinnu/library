The Slowlight Accord

Aboard the Strata, an orbital arcology circling a failing star, the founding Accord assigns every citizen a finite measure of wake-time. When an allocation runs out, the citizen enters Suspension. The First Signatories alone retain perpetual consciousness, debited against the common pool.

Quota Auditor Vey Corran discovers an impossible credit and follows it into the Original Allocation, a document sealed for three centuries. The scarcity used to justify mass Suspension may be an accounting choice rather than a physical necessity. To free the sleeping, Vey must make the institution reckon with a founding privilege it has learned to treat as natural law.
