The Inertial Compact

For two centuries, an orbital station has organized its society around the Halt, the moment its drive supposedly failed and stasis became a civic covenant. Junior compliance officer Lira Koss expects only signatures when she is assigned to verify the decade renewal.

Instead, she finds an unsealed annex containing a founder’s waiver—and evidence that the Inertial Engine never failed on its own. The institution Lira serves has been harvesting the difference between public sacrifice and private knowledge ever since. To place the truth on the ledger, she must navigate the procedures designed to make disclosure lawful, harmless, and impossible.
