The Tessellated Sky

Aboard a generation ship, citizens rebuild their identities each morning from the Weave, a distributed network that supplies every person with a fresh patch of communal memory. Sky-cartographer Esa Miro trusts the system—until an echo arrives bearing a star coordinate she never recorded.

The coordinate points to a pattern no official catalogue admits exists. As the anomaly repeats, Esa finds centuries of minute edits buried in the ship’s logs and evidence that the sky itself has been arranged to conceal a route. Following it means challenging the memory that holds the crew together; ignoring it may leave an entire civilization travelling inside a beautiful lie.
