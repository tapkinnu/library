The Migration of Burning Ships

Forty living starships carry sixty thousand sleeping colonists toward Vesper. Then every vessel turns away from the laser corridor and begins migrating toward Sable, a rogue gas giant erased from human charts.

Inside Morrowglass, ship surgeon Nara Kehl finds reproductive tissue growing through the navigation reef. Convoy Master Oren Vale has hours to restore the route before his authority fractures. Maintenance apprentice Syen Arq discovers that the ships have been preparing for this journey longer than any living crew has known. Captain Ilya Serin calls for a cull, certain that hesitation will repeat the biological catastrophe that killed her partner.

The truth is worse than sabotage. Humanity designed the ships to reproduce at Sable, promised them a path home, then buried the promise when living transport became too valuable to release. Now a hunter flotilla is closing on the nursery, the ships are molting around their passengers, and survival depends on negotiating with an intelligence that has no words for freedom, ownership, or mercy.
