The War of the Buried Sky

For ninety-six years, the rulers of Tern have kept the upper constellations fixed. Their Heartland harvests prosper while salt devours the Reed marshes and a second celestial vault presses upward. When the fortress of Ashwall rises from the earth and breaks, former orc general Kharza Olt carries the singing stone west as proof that the Great Turning can wait no longer.

The road to the four sky hinges crosses a realm choosing sides. Queen-Regent Osra Dane marches on Vael Tern with thirty-one thousand soldiers, determined to preserve the seasons of her childhood. Maela Ruun must decide whether defending the crown means destroying its oldest claim. Sevrin Ael follows failing bees toward a truth his own people may not forgive. Between them, goblin quartermaster Pell Vask keeps every bridge, refugee, and hidden cost from vanishing beneath heroic arithmetic.

Kharza cannot defeat Osra by gathering every people beneath one banner. Reed orc war-speaker Aruk Venn has reasons to fight for the regent and no intention of becoming subordinate. Across drowned roads, salt caverns, and the literal buried sky, alliances must be built from the right to refuse as much as the will to fight.
